# Auditing Subscriptions and Recurring Payments

OTT apps, music, cloud storage, gym memberships, food delivery memberships and app premium plans often renew silently through UPI AutoPay or saved cards.

## Do a 15-minute subscription audit

1. Open your UPI app's AutoPay / mandate section and your bank or card statement for the last three months.
2. List every recurring charge: Netflix, Amazon Prime, Disney+ Hotstar, JioCinema, Spotify, YouTube Premium, iCloud or Google One, gym, delivery memberships, edtech apps.
3. For each, ask: did I use this in the last 30 days? Would I pay for it again today?
4. Cancel or pause anything you would not buy again.

## Cheaper ways to keep what you use

- Check whether your mobile or broadband plan already bundles an OTT subscription.
- Use student plans where offered (some music and software services have student pricing with verification).
- Share family plans only as allowed by the service's terms.
- Rotate streaming services: subscribe for one month to watch a show, then cancel.
- Prefer monthly plans until you are sure you will use something all year.

## Control UPI AutoPay

You can view, pause or revoke UPI AutoPay mandates from the UPI app where you created them. Free trials that need a mandate will auto-convert into paid plans; set a calendar reminder before the trial ends.

## Track it

Mark recurring payments in BudgetIt under entertainment or utilities so their monthly total is visible.
