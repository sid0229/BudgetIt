# Credit Cards and CIBIL Score Basics for Young People

Your credit history decides whether you get loans, and on what terms, later in life. Starting well is easier than repairing it.

## What a credit score is

Credit bureaus in India (TransUnion CIBIL, Experian, Equifax and CRIF High Mark) build a credit report from your loans and cards. The CIBIL score ranges from 300 to 900; higher is better. Lenders, and sometimes employers or landlords, may check it. You are entitled to a free credit report from each bureau periodically; get it from the bureau's official website.

## What builds a good score

- **Paying every bill and EMI on time.** This matters most. Even one missed payment hurts.
- **Low credit utilisation**: using a small part of your card limit (many people aim to stay well under about 30%).
- **A longer history** and a healthy mix of credit over time.
- **Not applying for many loans or cards at once.**

## Using a credit card wisely

- **Always pay the total amount due**, not the "minimum amount due". Paying only the minimum triggers high interest on the remaining balance and on new purchases. Check your card's current interest rate and charges in the key fact statement.
- **Never withdraw cash** with a credit card; interest and fees start immediately.
- Set up auto-debit for the full amount from your savings account.
- Treat the card like a debit card: spend only money you already have.
- Beware EMI conversion offers; check processing fees and interest.

## Ways to start

- A secured credit card against a fixed deposit is a common first card for students and people with no credit history.
- An add-on card on a parent's account does not usually build your own history the same way.

## Protect your card

Never share CVV, OTP or PIN. Turn off international or online usage when not needed from your bank app. Report a lost card immediately.
