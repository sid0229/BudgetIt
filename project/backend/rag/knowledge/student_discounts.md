# Student Discounts and Freebies Worth Using

Your student ID and college email can save real money, as long as you only buy things you actually need.

## Where students commonly get discounts

- **Software and learning**: many software companies offer free or discounted student licences with a college email; GitHub's Student Developer Pack bundles many developer tools. Check each provider's official site for current offers.
- **Music and streaming**: some music services have student plans after verification.
- **Laptops and electronics**: brands sometimes run education pricing for students; compare with regular festive sales.
- **Travel**: student concessions exist on some railway, metro and state bus passes, and some airlines offer student fares; eligibility rules vary, so check the official website.
- **Museums, monuments and events**: student tickets are often cheaper with a valid ID.
- **Government learning platforms**: SWAYAM and NPTEL offer free online courses (certification exams may have a fee).
- **Libraries**: college and public libraries give free access to books, journals and sometimes e-resources you might otherwise buy.

## Use discounts without overspending

- A discount on something you did not plan to buy is still spending.
- Compare the "student price" with sale prices before buying.
- Do not share your ID or college login with others to get them discounts.
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails that push you into impulse buys.

## Track what you save

Note the money saved through discounts in BudgetIt and move that amount to a savings goal so the saving is real.
