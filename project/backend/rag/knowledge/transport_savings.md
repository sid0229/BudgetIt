# Cutting Transport Costs in Indian Cities

Daily commuting to college, coaching or office can add up quickly, especially with app-based cabs and autos.

## Compare your options honestly

- **Metro, local trains and city buses** are usually the cheapest per kilometre. Monthly passes, smart cards or student concession passes reduce cost further where available; check your city transport authority or railway website for current eligibility.
- **Shared autos and bike taxis** (such as Rapido) are often cheaper than cabs for short distances.
- **Uber/Ola cabs** are convenient but surge pricing during rain, peak hours and late nights can make a single ride cost as much as a week of metro travel.

## Habits that save money

- Plan the first and last mile: walk or cycle short stretches instead of booking an auto.
- Avoid booking during surge; wait 10 to 15 minutes or take the metro part of the way.
- Carpool with classmates or colleagues and split fuel costs.
- If you own a two-wheeler, maintain correct tyre pressure and regular servicing to improve mileage, and keep insurance and PUC certificates valid to avoid fines.
- Choose accommodation close to college, office or a metro station; slightly higher rent can be cheaper overall than long daily cab rides.

## Track it

Set a weekly transport budget in BudgetIt. If cab spending is more than your pass would cost, switch to the pass.

## Long-distance travel

Book train tickets early on IRCTC, compare bus fares, and use student or senior concessions only where you are genuinely eligible.
