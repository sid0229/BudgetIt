# Banking Basics: Savings Accounts, Debit Cards and Complaints

## Choosing a savings account

- Check the minimum balance requirement; falling below it leads to charges. Zero-balance accounts, including Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts (BSBDA), avoid this.
- Look at charges for ATM withdrawals beyond free limits, SMS alerts, debit card fees and cheque books in the bank's schedule of charges.
- Keep the number of accounts small; dormant accounts with charges are a waste.
- Update KYC only through the bank's official branch, app or website.

## Debit cards and ATMs

- Cover the keypad when entering your PIN and check the ATM for skimming devices.
- Use your bank app to set limits and switch off international or online transactions you do not need.
- Report a lost card immediately through the official app or helpline.

## Unauthorised transactions

Under RBI rules, your liability for unauthorised electronic transactions can be limited or zero if you report quickly to your bank. Report as soon as you notice, and keep the complaint reference number. For fraud, also call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.

## Complaints

1. Complain to your bank first through its official grievance channel.
2. If not resolved within the allowed time, or you are unhappy with the reply, file a complaint under the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme at cms.rbi.org.in. This is free.

## Useful habits

- Check your statement every month.
- Set up nomination on every account and deposit.
- Never share net banking passwords, OTPs or PINs; banks will never ask for them.

*This is educational information, not financial advice.*
