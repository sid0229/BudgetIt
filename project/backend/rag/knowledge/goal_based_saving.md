# Goal-Based Saving

Saving is easier when the money has a name: "laptop", "Goa trip", "GRE fee", "first month's rent deposit".

## Set SMART goals

Make each goal Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound. "Save ₹30,000 for a laptop by 30 June" is much better than "save more".

## Work out the monthly or weekly amount

Divide the remaining amount by the time left:

- Goal: ₹30,000 laptop in 10 months, already saved ₹5,000.
- Remaining: ₹25,000 / 10 months = ₹2,500 per month, or roughly ₹580 per week.

If that is too much, extend the deadline, lower the target (a refurbished laptop), or find extra income.

## Match the goal to the right place

- **Under one year**: savings account, RD or short FD. Do not risk short-term money in the stock market.
- **One to three years**: RD, FD or debt-oriented options.
- **Five years or more**: equity index fund SIPs may suit part of the money if you accept the ups and downs.

## Make progress automatic

- Open a separate account, RD or "pot" per major goal.
- Automate a transfer on payday or the day pocket money arrives.
- Round up: move small leftovers at month end into the goal.
- Celebrate milestones (25%, 50%, 75%) to stay motivated.

## Priority order

1. Small emergency fund.
2. Avoid or clear high-interest debt (credit card dues, BNPL late fees).
3. Near-term essential goals (fees, deposits).
4. Wants (trips, gadgets) and long-term investing.
